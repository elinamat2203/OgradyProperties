What we do

We help with sales, lettings and valuations across Sligo.

Providing straightforward, practical advice based on local knowledge and experience. From your first call through to the final details, we keep you informed, handle the process carefully and make moving, letting or valuing your property as simple and stress-free as possible.

Sales

We help prepare your property for market, arrange photography and presentation, advertise across our website and social media, manage viewings, handle offers and negotiate to achieve the best possible result.

Property Management

We take the stress out of managing your rental property. We find and reference reputable tenants, manage rent collection, inspections and maintenance, and handle day-to-day tenant communication, giving you complete peace of mind while we take care of your property.

Valuations

We provide professional valuations for mortgage, probate, legal purposes and prospective sales. Our experienced team combines local knowledge and current market conditions to provide an accurate and reliable assessment of your property's value.

About Us

Established in 1996 by Bryan Corcoran, the business quickly became one of Sligo's most trusted independent estate agencies, earning a reputation for professionalism, integrity and outstanding customer service.

For almost 30 years, Bryan J Corcoran Auctioneers has successfully assisted clients with residential, commercial and land sales, valuations, lettings and property management.

Having worked alongside Bryan and Mary for many years, Martell O'Grady has proudly taken over the business, which now trades as Martell O'Grady Estate Agents. The new name reflects the firm's continued evolution while maintaining the same values, local knowledge and personal service that clients have trusted since 1996.

Our values are founded on honesty, integrity and exceptional customer service, ensuring a seamless property experience for buyers, sellers, landlords and tenants throughout County Sligo.

Supported by Property Administrator & Sales Consultant Elina Mat, our team combines decades of local experience with modern marketing techniques to ensure every client receives a professional, responsive and personal service.
